Some posts can't be shown

The card says so, instead of pretending otherwise.

🔒 Private or restricted
A protected account, a friends-only post, or a private, banned or quarantined community. Logged-out is logged-out.
🔞 Age-gated
Needs a signed-in adult account. On YouTube it is the thumbnail and no playback, and that one is permanent rather than worth retrying.
🎬 Video came back as a still
Carousels with video flatten to the cover. Instagram reels using major-label audio arrive with no video at all. Anything over 20 minutes or 300 MB isn't remuxed.
😕 "Couldn't load this post"
The reason isn't knowable. On Threads, private, deleted and age-gated look identical from outside, so I don't guess.
